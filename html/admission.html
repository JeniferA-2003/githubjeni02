Admission

Admission to all courses is based on the merit, category of the candidate, and the availability of seats at the time of counselling. All admissions made by CENTAC are “provisional” and are subject to approval by Pondicherry University. Candidates who have been offered admission to a particular College should report to that College on or before the due date mentioned by CENTAC , in the ‘provisional admission order’, failing which, they will forfeit the right of admission.At any time, if the CENTAC / College Authorities find that admission has been offered based on false / incorrect information / documents and suppression of relevant fact, education qualification or quota, the admission offered will be summarily cancelled, irrespective of the stage of study of that course. Further, legal proceedings will also be initiated against such candidates for such actions. Hence, candidates are warned against indulging in such actions, in their own interest. All parents are also requested to make note of the above provision, and take due care and diligence, in all matter concerning admission to the undergraduate courses.

How To Apply

Procedure For Apply

Application forms can be obtained from any one of the six colleges under CAPASC by paying Rs. 100/= in person or Rs. 120/= by post by sending Money Order in the name of The Convenor, CAPASC, Bharathidasan Govt. College for Women, Muthialpet, Puducherry – 605003.

The application forms completed in all respects and with all necessary certificates, etc. (as specified in the application form) should be submitted to The Convenor, CAPASC, Bharathidasan Govt. College for Women, Muthialpet, Puducherry – 605003.

Candidates who appeared for the supplementary examination should not wait for the results but submit the Application Form to The Convenor, CAPASC on or before the last date. However such candidates should submit the attested photo copy of the mark statement of the qualifying examination within Five days from the date of issue of mark sheet.

Candidates for admission may submit the prescribed application duly furnishing all the relevant particulars with regard to Residence, Nativity and Community with a declaration to the effect that all the particulars furnished by them are true and if any information furnished by them is found to be false at a later stage, it would render them liable for severe action. The candidate may however submit their original certificates in support of their residence, nativity and community at the time of counselling.


  • Application may also be sent registered post addressed to The Convenor, CAPASC so as, to reach the Convenor on or before the last date specified

  • The application number should be written in all the enclosures sent along with the main application form.

  • Candidates applying for different courses in different colleges should send only one application.

  • Multiple applications will summarily be rejected.

As soon as the applications are received atCAPASC office, intimation will be sent to the applicants giving theregistration number, which should be quoted in all subsequent correspondence regarding admission. Enquiries will not be attended to unless the registration number is quoted.
